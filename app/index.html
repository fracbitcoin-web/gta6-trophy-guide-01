Seven stages, built from the official descriptions. Play the story first, then the character strands, then activities and open-world crime, and finish with collectibles and cleanup. The platinum pops when the other 54 are done.

    Sources & honesty note

    Official names, descriptions and grades come from the official trophy list as published on Exophase and PSNProfiles (28 of the 55 are hidden on PlayStation until earned). Every guide on this page is written in our own words from those descriptions. Grand Theft Auto VI had not released when this guide was written, so nothing beyond the official description is stated as fact: where a guide says “inferred”, it is our reading of the description, not a confirmed step. Counts such as 42 Shooting Range challenges or 14 drone-race locations are PSN’s own tracked targets. No Rockstar artwork, logo or game footage is used anywhere here — the look is our own.
